## W3C vocabularies

- **SKOS:** `serialisation/imports/skos.rdf` is the unchanged source download from <https://www.w3.org/2009/08/skos-reference/skos.rdf>. `skos-reference.ttl` selects named terms, axioms and documentation for local import. It omits unused collection/list constructs and retains the original term IRIs. See the [SKOS Reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/) and its copyright and use notices.
- **PROV-O:** `serialisation/imports/prov-o.ttl` is the unchanged source download from <https://www.w3.org/ns/prov-o.ttl>. `prov-reference.ttl` selects supporting axioms and documentation for Agent, Entity, Collection and InstantaneousEvent, retaining their original term IRIs. See [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) and its copyright and use notices.

The local reference subsets are adaptations for import compatibility, not the complete W3C vocabularies. External term definitions remain attributable to W3C and their original contributors; they are not relicensed as iCARE Onto content. Preserve upstream notices when redistributing them. W3C maintains its [copyright and licensing information](https://www.w3.org/copyright/); the applicable source notices govern.
